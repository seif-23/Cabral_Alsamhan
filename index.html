About Us

What Sets Us Apart

Practice Areas

Cabral Alsamhan PLLC provides attorney-led representation across immigration and business-related legal matters for international professionals, entrepreneurs, investors, and globally mobile clients.

EB-2 NIW & Employment-Based Immigration

Strategic representation for professionals seeking employment-based green cards, including EB-2 National Interest Waivers and direct sponsorship pathways.

E-2 Treaty Investor & L-1 Transfers

Visa solutions for foreign investors and multinational companies establishing or expanding U.S. operations through treaty investor visas and intracompany transfers.

H-1B & Employment-Based Immigration

Comprehensive support for skilled professionals seeking temporary work authorization and pathways to permanent residence through employment sponsorship.

O-1 Visa & EB-1 Extraordinary Ability

Immigration pathways for individuals with extraordinary ability in sciences, arts, business, and athletics, including nonimmigrant and permanent residence options.

Business Law & Cross-Border Legal Services

Legal support for U.S. business formation, corporate structuring, business agreements, and international transactions for foreign entrepreneurs.

Employment Authorization & Work Visas

Strategic representation for professionals seeking work permits, employment authorization documents, and visa options aligned with career objectives.

Family-Based Immigration

Streamlined pathways for family reunification with strategic case planning, visa petitions, and adjustment of status or consular processing.

Citizenship & Naturalization

Guidance through the naturalization process, citizenship applications, and resolution of eligibility issues for permanent residents seeking U.S. citizenship.

Humanitarian & Protective Immigration Relief

Comprehensive representation for asylum seekers, VAWA petitions, U-visa holders, and individuals requiring humanitarian protection and relief.

Criminal & Immigration Consequences

Integrated analysis of criminal charges and immigration implications, with strategic defense in removal proceedings and immigration court cases.

Strategic & Complex Filings

Attorney-led representation for immigration matters requiring advanced legal positioning, individualized documentation strategy, and highly tailored case development. Every complex case receives Dr. Al-Samhan's personal attention and strategic oversight.
